Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How does your "land first, then project work" process work?

We start by identifying and handing over the land first — with a clear title and complete documentation. Once the land is with you, we carry out the solar, wind, or farmhouse development work depending on what your project needs.

How long does land handover usually take?

Timelines depend on the land's documentation status, size, and location. We discuss a realistic, honest timeline with you during the first site consultation rather than quoting a generic number upfront.

Is my investment and the land title legally secure?

Transparency is central to how we work — clear titles, verified documentation, and milestones are shared with every stakeholder before any commitment is made. We encourage you to have your own legal advisor review documentation as well.

Can I purchase or lease land through Karam Land Promoters?

Yes. We support both purchase and lease arrangements, structured around whether your project is solar, wind, or farmhouse development.

Can I visit the site before deciding?

Yes, site visits are welcome and encouraged. Use the form on this page or call us directly to schedule a convenient time.

Which regions do you operate in?

We're based in Arakkonam and are active across Vadamambakkam and the surrounding high-growth corridors in Tamil Nadu.

What's the minimum land size or investment needed?

This varies by project type and location. Share your requirement and rough budget in the form below, and we'll respond with specific guidance for your case.

I'm interested in a job — how do I apply?

Check our Careers page for current openings, or send your CV through the form on this page and select "Career / job application" from the dropdown.
