Who we are

Karam Land Promoters is a Tamil Nadu–based company whose model is simple: we provide the land, then handle solar, wind, and farmhouse project work for clients who need ready sites and reliable execution.

We work with landowners, investors, and EPC partners — first on land identification, papers, and handover; then on the development work that turns each site into a solar park, wind corridor, or premium farm estate.

Proprietor N. Antony leads a team rooted in the Arakkonam region, with deep knowledge of local land records, connectivity, and regulatory pathways.
